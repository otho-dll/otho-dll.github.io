## What exactly is it?
As said before this is an extension for [Obsidian](https://obsidian.md) that allows creation of handwritten notes. The idea is simple but every other extension I found did not fit my expectations hence creation of this plugin. It very simple in it's form, just a "new" file format - .hnote, which is basically a renamed .json, everything is stored in it and rendered as pages on screen. I doesn't like the "infinite canvas" stuff, that's why my solution has regular pages, just like a notebook would have.
